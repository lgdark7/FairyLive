• Single-App Isolation Architecture:
◦ Implemented CaptureSource.SINGLE_APP utilizing Android 14+ / 15 MediaProjectionConfig task picker (RECORD_CONTENT_TASK).

◦ Result: The virtual display binds strictly to the chosen application's task ID. FAIRY LIVE's studio UI, floating HUD, system navigation bars, notification shades, and incoming calls are completely excluded from the video stream at the OS compositor level.
• Entire Screen Fallback:
◦ For legacy devices or creators who explicitly configure full-screen streaming, CaptureSource.ENTIRE_SCREEN uses MediaProjectionConfig.createConfigForDefaultDisplay().
