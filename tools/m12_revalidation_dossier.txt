BLOCKER 6: CONTROLLER COORDINATION & PRECEDENCE HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To eliminate controller hunting, feedback loops, or oscillation between M9 Network ABR, M12 Thermal Mitigation, and M12 Dynamic FPS Throttler, a strict mathematical hierarchy is enforced:
1. Precedence Principles:
   - Thermal Mitigation establishes physical performance ceilings: `thermalBitrateCeiling` and `thermalFpsCeiling`.
   - Network ABR (`AdaptiveBitrateController`) governs bitrate within `[300, thermalBitrateCeiling]` based on network socket health. It cannot upscale past the thermal ceiling during network recovery.
   - Dynamic FPS Throttler governs compositor presentation cadence within `[15, thermalFpsCeiling]` based on actual GLES render latency. It cannot upscale past the thermal FPS ceiling during low-latency frames.
2. Mathematical Invariants:
   - Active Bitrate = min(ABR Evaluated Bitrate, Thermal Bitrate Ceiling)
   - Active Frame Rate = min(Dynamic Render FPS, Thermal FPS Ceiling)
3. Verified Scenarios (`LowEndOptimizationTest`):
   - `testAbrThermalCeilingEnforcementAndRecovery`: ABR drops from 1200 to 800 kbps under MODERATE thermal ceiling. Sustained EXCELLENT network evaluations are clamped at 800 kbps. When thermal level returns to NOMINAL, ABR smoothly recovers to 1200 kbps.
   - `testDynamicFpsThrottlerThermalCeilingPreventsOverRecovery`: Throttler clamped under MODERATE thermal ceiling (24 FPS) cannot recover to 30 FPS even when render latency drops < 10ms.
   - `testCombinedControllerPrecedence`: Combined network degradation and thermal escalation operate with zero oscillation.
