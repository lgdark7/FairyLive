3. THERMAL BITRATE LADDER HARMONIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Explanation of Why the Thermal Ladder Changed:
- Historical Context:
  * When M12 was initially scoped, a provisional thermal degradation ladder was proposed: [1200, 900, 700, 500 kbps].
  * However, Milestone 9 had already formally defined and approved the production `AdaptiveBitrateController` discrete ladder as: [1200, 800, 500, 300 kbps] with a strict broadcast floor of 300 kbps.
- Architectural Reason for Harmonization:
  * In the previous review, ChatGPT required explicit controller coordination to prevent oscillation between M9 Network ABR and M12 Thermal Mitigation.
  * If Thermal Mitigation operated on [1200, 900, 700, 500] while ABR operated on [1200, 800, 500, 300], the two controllers would command mismatched bitrate steps, causing rounding ambiguity, conflicting encoder commands, or attempts to upscale past thermal limits.
  * To achieve clean mathematical coordination, the thermal mitigation ceiling rungs were intentionally aligned 1:1 with M9's approved discrete ABR ladder:
    - NOMINAL  (Thermal Status 0/1) -> 1200 kbps target ceiling (ABR Tier 0) | 30 FPS target
    - MODERATE (Thermal Status 2)   -> 800 kbps target ceiling  (ABR Tier 1) | 24 FPS target
    - SEVERE   (Thermal Status 3)   -> 500 kbps target ceiling  (ABR Tier 2) | 20 FPS target
    - CRITICAL (Thermal Status 4+)  -> 300 kbps target ceiling  (ABR Tier 3) | 15 FPS target
  * Compatibility: Fully compatible with M9's 300 kbps floor. ABR operates dynamically within the thermal ceiling:
    `Active Bitrate = min(ABR Evaluated Bitrate, Thermal Bitrate Ceiling)`
    `Active FPS = min(Render Latency Target FPS, Thermal FPS Ceiling)`
  * Documented across: PROJECT_MEMORY.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, PERFORMANCE.md, DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md, REQUIREMENTS.md, and TESTING.md.
