3. ARCHITECTURAL OPTIMIZATIONS IMPLEMENTED (`com.fairylive.app.core.optimization`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. `ThermalMitigationManager.kt`:
   - Listens to Android system thermal status via `PowerManager.OnThermalStatusChangedListener` (API 29+) with backward-compatible battery temperature fallback.
   - Reactive 4-Tier Mitigation Ladder:
     * NOMINAL (Status 0/1): 30 FPS target, 1200 kbps bitrate.
     * MODERATE (Status 2): 24 FPS target, 900 kbps bitrate.
     * SEVERE (Status 3): 20 FPS target, 700 kbps bitrate, disabled non-critical animations.
     * CRITICAL / EMERGENCY (Status 4+): 15 FPS target, 500 kbps bitrate, absolute CPU/GPU conservation.
   - Automatic Recovery: Smoothly restores nominal FPS and bitrate when sensors cool down, without dropping or restarting the live RTMP broadcast.

2. `MemoryPressureGuard.kt` & `OverlayResourceManager.trimMemory()`:
   - Registers `ComponentCallbacks2` in `FairyStreamService`.
   - On `TRIM_MEMORY_RUNNING_LOW`: Prunes oldest 50% of cached overlay bitmap textures.
   - On `TRIM_MEMORY_RUNNING_CRITICAL`: Completely evicts 100% of cached bitmap textures and issues a proactive `System.gc()` hint to prevent OS kill.

3. `DynamicFpsThrottler.kt`:
   - Zero-allocation rolling 10-frame render latency monitor embedded inside `OverlayCompositor`.
   - If average frame draw latency exceeds 32ms, it steps down render target (30 -> 24 -> 20 -> 15 FPS) to protect compositor queue depth and prevent frame drop cascading.
   - Automatically steps back up to 30 FPS once render latency settles below 18ms for 30 consecutive frames.

4. `DeviceProfileDetector.kt`:
   - Automatically probes total RAM (`ActivityManager.getMemoryInfo`), core count, and `ActivityManager.isLowRamDevice`.
   - Classifies device into `LOW_END`, `MID_RANGE`, or `FLAGSHIP`, configuring tailored streaming profiles at startup.
