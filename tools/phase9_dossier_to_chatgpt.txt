### P0 — Overlay Interaction Stability Root Cause & Resolution
1. 4-Corner Resize Inversion:
   - Root Cause: `GestureState.RESIZE` in `BroadcastPreviewView.kt` previously assumed all drag deltas originated from the bottom-right corner, ignoring `activeCorner` (0: TL, 1: TR, 2: BR, 3: BL), causing top/left drags to invert resizing deltas and jump.
   - Fix: Implemented corner-aware coordinate origin compensation across all 4 corners with local-space delta unrotation (`unrotateDelta()`).
2. Drag Snapping Magnet Jerk:
   - Root Cause: 2.5% snapping threshold magnetically trapped elements during small drag motions.
   - Fix: Refined snapping threshold to 1.0% width and 0.8% margins for smooth breakout.
3. Cardinal Rotation Snapping:
   - Added 3.5° snap lock to 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°.
