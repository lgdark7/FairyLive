2. `AdaptiveBitrateController` required **3 consecutive evaluation windows** (3000ms) of healthy status before stepping:
   - `300 kbps -> 500 kbps` (`reason=SUSTAINED_HEALTHY_RECOVERY`)
   - Cooldown held for 3000ms.
   - `500 kbps -> 800 kbps` (`reason=SUSTAINED_HEALTHY_RECOVERY`)
   - Cooldown held for 3000ms.
   - `800 kbps -> 1200 kbps` (`reason=SUSTAINED_HEALTHY_RECOVERY`)

## 8. Network Transport Transition Status
- **Implemented**: `NetworkQualityMonitor.kt` registers `ConnectivityManager.NetworkCallback` with `NetworkCapabilities.NET_CAPABILITY_INTERNET`, dynamically updating `currentTransport` (`WIFI`, `CELLULAR`, `ETHERNET`, `UNAVAILABLE`) and triggering health re-evaluation on network loss.
- **AVD Limitation & Scope Note**: **Physical Wi-Fi <-> Cellular live streaming migration was not validated in M9.** The Android Studio emulator environment routes traffic through a single virtualized Wi-Fi/Ethernet bridge. True cellular multi-homing handover will be tested when physical multi-SIM devices are provisioned.
