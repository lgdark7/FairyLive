## 2. Audio HAL Gap Silence Recovery Verification

- **Mechanism**: Pure-Kotlin `AudioCaptureEngine` monitors elapsed wall-clock session PTS against sample-accumulated PTS. When an audio driver/HAL stall occurs exceeding `GAP_COMPENSATION_THRESHOLD_US` (40ms), the engine synthesizes zeroed PCM frames (`ByteArray(minBufferSize)`) matching the exact missing frame count (capped at 3.0 seconds per event).
- **Test Results**:
  - `Milestone13StressUnitTest.kt` (`testAudioHalGapSilenceSynthesisRecovery`): Verified 350ms simulated stall synthesizes 15,435 silence frames across 8 chunk dispatches, preserving continuous micro-spacing and monotonic PTS progression.
  - `Milestone13StressInstrumentedTest.kt` (`testAudioHalGapSilenceSynthesisRecoveryOnDevice`): Executed on Pixel 8 emulator; verified 250ms simulated stall synthesizes 11,025 silence frames with 100% monotonic timestamps and zero pipeline stalls.
  - **Verdict**: PASS. Audio/video synchronization remains locked without timestamp jumps or dropped audio packets.

## 3. Codec Resource Boundary Enforcement

- **Policy**: Fairy Live enforces an immutable architectural ceiling of at most **2 active hardware/software MediaCodec instances** during active live broadcast:
  1. **Video Encoder**: `HardwareVideoEncoder` (`c2.android.avc.encoder` on emulator / platform hardware encoder on device).
  2. **Audio Encoder**: `AacMediaCodecEncoder` (`c2.android.aac.encoder`).
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Camera PIP (Facecam) is rendered directly via zero-copy OpenGL ES `SurfaceTexture` quad blending onto the primary compositor surface, requiring **0 additional video codecs**.
  - Animated GIFs and storage image assets are decoded into memory via `Movie` / downsampled `BitmapFactory`, requiring **0 additional video codecs**.
  - All 10 cycles of the lifecycle stress test and the 30-minute soak test confirm that active codec count never exceeded 2.
