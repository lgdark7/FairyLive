2. DETAILED PHASE 4 IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ARBITRARY GEOMETRY & ROTATION (0°–360°):
- Full support for arbitrary element rotation in OverlayTransform, BroadcastPreviewView, and GlesRenderer.
- Matrix math in GlesRenderer handles rotation, scaling, translation, and pivot points.
- Interactive multi-touch two-finger rotation gesture and inspector angle slider/presets (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°).

B. ARBITRARY RECTANGULAR & SHAPE CROPPING (MaskShape):
- Integrated CropRegion normalized boundaries (cropLeft, cropTop, cropRight, cropBottom) into WebOverlayConfig.
- Added MaskShape support (RECTANGLE, ROUNDED_RECT, CIRCLE) with cornerRadiusPx.
- Implemented non-destructive bitmap crop in OffscreenWebRenderer and OpenGL ES shader texture coordinate mapping.

C. HEADLESS WEB OVERLAY RASTERIZER (OffscreenWebRenderer):
- Pure offscreen WebView rasterization directly to ARGB_8888 Bitmaps without attaching to on-screen view trees.
- Programmatic scroll offsets (scrollX, scrollY) and zoom level (setInitialScale) targeting specific sub-regions.
- CSS and JS injection via evaluateJavascript for widget styling and removing extraneous navigation headers.
- Transparent canvas support with hardware canvas lock/unlock for zero-copy bitmap extraction.

D. ZERO-LEAK RENDER DOMAIN ISOLATION:
- Strict segregation of BROADCAST_COMPOSITE (game/screen capture + transformed overlays -> GLES -> MediaCodec AVC -> RTMP) and STREAMER_HUD_UI (WindowManager.addView TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY).
- Streamer HUD, chat bubbles, health counters, studio canvas controls, and inspectors NEVER leak into the broadcast stream.
- Verified on Android 15 emulator with live screencap tools/v11_phase4_broadcast.flv.

E. STREAMER FLOATING HUD ENHANCEMENTS:
- Facecam toggle, Mic mute toggle, Chat HUD, and Pause/Freeze controls.
- Dynamic drag-to-dock physics and non-intrusive floating pills.
