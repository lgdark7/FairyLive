2. CODEC LIFECYCLE EVIDENCE
==================================================
Investigation of HardwareVideoEncoder.kt and AacMediaCodecEncoder.kt confirms:
- Codecs are NOT continuously retained across stream sessions.
- In HardwareVideoEncoder.stop():
    mediaCodec?.stop()
    mediaCodec?.release()
    mediaCodec = null
    inputSurface?.release()
    inputSurface = null
- In AacMediaCodecEncoder.stop():
    mediaCodec?.stop()
    mediaCodec?.release()
    mediaCodec = null
- Lifecycle Reality: MediaCodec instances are cleanly stopped, released, and set to null upon session teardown, and newly initialized upon capture start.
- Verified Invariant: "Active codec count remained bounded at 2 (1 AVC video, 1 AAC audio) at every measurement point during active streaming." Zero zombie or accumulated codec instances exist across start/stop cycles.
