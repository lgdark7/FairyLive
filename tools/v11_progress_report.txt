# FAIRY LIVE V1.1 "Creator Control & Live Widgets" Progress Dossier

## 1. Architectural Foundation & Render-Domain Isolation
We have successfully implemented the first major wave of **FAIRY LIVE V1.1**:
- **Render-Domain Isolation Established (`RenderDomain.kt`)**:
  - `RenderDomain.BROADCAST`: Broadcast-composited elements entering GLES compositor -> MediaCodec AVC -> RTMP stream.
  - `RenderDomain.CREATOR_ONLY`: Components rendered exclusively on the creator's device view hierarchy (Window/DecorView), strictly forbidden from entering BroadcastScene or the GLES compositor.
- **Web / Browser Overlay Models & Rasterizers**:
  - Defined `WebOverlayConfig` supporting transparent backgrounds, refresh intervals, and fallback placeholders (`WebOverlayRasterizer`).
  - Added `OverlayType.WEB` across `OverlayElement`, `GlesRenderer`, and `SceneRepository`.
- **YouTube Live Subscriber Counter Widget**:
  - Created `YouTubeSubscriberWidgetConfig` and high-performance offscreen rasterizer `YouTubeSubscriberRasterizer` (producing 480x160 broadcast-ready textures).
  - Integrated into `sheet_add_overlay.xml` as a dedicated item card under Stream Interactive.
  - Interactive canvas & Creator Studio wired to instantiate, configure, and inspect YouTube subscriber widgets.
- **Streamer Floating HUD (`StreamerFloatingHudManager.kt`)**:
  - Non-intrusive floating draggable pill dock rendered in `RenderDomain.CREATOR_ONLY`.
  - Mic mute/unmute control directly controlling `AudioCaptureEngine` & `AudioPipeline` to ensure zero PCM leakage while preserving PTS timestamp monotonicity.
  - Stream pause/resume control freezing video capture truthfully without tearing down the RTMP socket.
  - Creator-only Live Chat bottom sheet (`sheet_creator_chat.xml`) with chat view and reply composer.
