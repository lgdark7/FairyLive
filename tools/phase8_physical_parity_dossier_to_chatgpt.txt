## 3. P0 — SINGLE-APP CAPTURE & CREATOR ISOLATION
- **Task Isolation**: Routing to `RECORD_CONTENT_TASK` restricts capture strictly to the target application task window.
- **Creator Controls Segregation**:
  - Selection handles, corner grips, crop notches, and rotation pins are drawn exclusively on `BroadcastPreviewView.onDraw(canvas)` and never pass into `GlesRenderer.kt`.
  - Floating HUD is bound to `RenderDomain.CREATOR_ONLY` and suppressed during studio canvas editing.
  - Compositor renders only overlays with `domain == RenderDomain.BROADCAST`.
