### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Milestone 15-F has been successfully implemented and verified on the Pixel 8 Android 15 emulator. The Creator Studio UX has been restructured from a vertically scrolling layout to an immersive, Canvas-First viewport. The 16:9 broadcast canvas remains permanently visible in dedicated upper screen space, while contextual editing is powered by a non-intrusive action bar and a half-height (45% peek, 50% half-expanded ratio) expandable BottomSheetBehavior.
